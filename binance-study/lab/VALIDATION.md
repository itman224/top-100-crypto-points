# Independent validation

## Existing input data

The existing event extraction covers 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-27 UTC. `output/summary.json` reports 100 symbols processed, 96 complete, and 6,046,848 of 6,048,000 expected native 5-minute candles (99.98095%). VVVUSDT, WIFUSDT, XANUSDT, and ZECUSDT each lack the entire 2026-10-27 day; missing is not a zero-event observation. The source universe is conditional on an active third-party symbol snapshot and 509 ranked candidates, so it can omit delisted contracts. The manifest records 899 source URLs as inferred and 3,246 as verified. The existing validation report marks 17 legacy symbol-months as pending original close-timestamp validation and records untracked fallback transfer bytes; these affect provenance confidence, not a claim of verified missing candles.

Independently scanned all 800 event JSONL gzip chunks: 463,114 events. Every 1,009th event (458 rows) was independently recomputed as `100*(close/open-1)`; all sampled rows exceeded 1% in absolute value, matched their recorded percentage within 1e-8, and were marked complete. This is a sample audit, not a re-run of the original raw candles.

## Required interpretation

The event files contain only qualifying candles, so they are unsuitable as the sole benchmark input. Classification metrics require complete non-event controls from continuous data; missing observations and warm-up bars must not be counted as negatives. Signals evaluated at a candle close must be shifted to a subsequent executable entry; no same-close fill may be assumed. Chronological train/validation/holdout boundaries must not permit data-dependent parameter choices on holdout. Use enough embargo or position closure to prevent outcomes crossing a split. Gaps must reset rolling history and invalidate incomplete larger candles.

Gross and net results must distinguish fee/slippage assumptions and missing funding. A candle crossing both stop and target cannot reveal path order; conservative stop-first handling or an explicit unresolved result is necessary. A per-symbol sequential backtest does not establish portfolio-level returns, Sharpe, risk, or capital allocation if simultaneous symbols are combined without an explicit capital and overlap model. Precision and recall must be defined independently for UP and DOWN versus all other valid examples and reported with base rates, support, and uncertainty; large correlated candle counts do not constitute independent samples. Position overlap, exposure, annualization, small samples, multiple testing, universe survivorship and broad market correlation all constrain apparent statistical significance.

## Research run review

The first BTCUSDT research run generated 690 per-symbol/timeframe/split/direction records under `one-candle-v3`, with a SHA-256 experiment hash that matched the current `backtest.py`, `indicators.py`, and `data.py` at review. The loaded continuous data reported 132,480 valid 5-minute bars including 250 warm-up days, with no missing bars. The execution period alone remains 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-27.

Independent reconciliation of BTC 5-minute baseline labels found 16 bullish events (10 train, 4 validation, 2 holdout) and 14 bearish events (7, 2, 5), matching the 30 BTC 5-minute events in the prior output. Synthetic tests for +1% and -1% threshold boundaries, delayed execution, gap invalidation, and causal features passed. Decimal re-evaluation of boundary-adjacent float prices is a safeguard but cannot restore precision already lost during CSV-to-float conversion.

Inspected strategy code computes signals on completed candles, shifts them one bar, and simulates the next candle open-to-close. Split boundaries are April 1 / July 1 / September 1 / October 28, and trades close within the same target candle. Indicator computations are restarted at timestamp gaps. No material lookahead was found in this inspected path; this does not independently certify all source files or validate future changes. Ten current lab unit tests passed in the independent run.

The assumed next-bar-open fill is idealized: a real signal computed at the previous close may not execute at exactly the next open. The study uses 10 basis points per round trip and excludes funding. No TP/SL, intrabar path, MFE/MAE, shared portfolio capital, latency, market impact, or multiple-testing correction is simulated. Sharpe/Sortino are unannualized per-trade metrics; pooled trades are correlated and worst symbol-segment drawdown is not a portfolio drawdown. Results therefore do not establish deployable or statistically robust profitability. Candidate selection currently ranks train positive mean with at least 30 trades before reading validation and holdout; a failed validation candidate is not a qualified strategy even if shown with its holdout result. Train-selected holdout results are not evidence for tuning.

The BTC-only results are a pilot, not the requested 100-symbol benchmark. A full result requires successful per-symbol research processing, aggregation, and review of missing data; no strategy has yet been independently certified as profitable or stable.

## Independent review of pooled results (partial, six symbols)

At the time of this review `results/results.json` held six symbols, 690 pooled records, 16 train-selected candidate configurations, six of which passed the validation gate; one of these six also met the exploratory holdout threshold. These counts describe only that snapshot, not the completed 100-symbol study or a recommendation. Reconciled all 690 aggregate confusion matrices: `TP + FP + FN + TN == valid_samples`, `TP + FP == trades`, and wins never exceeded trades. Code inspection found that pooled precision, recall, moments and profit factor derive from summed underlying counts/moments, and per-trade Sharpe is not labeled a portfolio return.

The implemented selection ranks candidates by train mean return and trade count, applies a validation gate, and exposes holdout only for candidates passing validation; no automated ranking on holdout was found. However, holdout metrics have been visible while symbol coverage and pooled training selections evolve, so the eventual holdout should be described as exploratory rather than a pristine confirmatory experiment. Freeze universe, strategy definitions, selection and code version before a future untouched evaluation. A minimum of 30 trades, positive mean and profit factor > 1 is not a robustness test; no multiplicity adjustment, confidence intervals, or monthly/cross-symbol stability test has been established. Trade-weighted pooled results overrepresent high-activity symbols and correlated observations; they cannot support a portfolio performance claim without explicit position sizing and simultaneous capital constraints. The insight comparisons of volume filters, regime filters and timeframes are descriptive, not causal matched experiments. Cross-timeframe confirmation, hour/regime analysis and funding are marked not tested rather than inferred.

## Full-universe review and October 27 repair

The research run subsequently processed all 100 selected symbols. All 100 symbol result files matched the current code-version hash and selected universe. Across the 690 pooled records, independent recomputation from the per-symbol records found no mismatches in confusion counts, trade counts, wins, or summed gross and net returns. Source quality reported zero invalid rows or duplicates. The run reported no missing 5-minute bars within the requested analysis dates; warm-up history outside that range had 805,341 missing 5-minute bars across the 100 symbols and rolling calculations require fresh observations after each gap.

The four October 27 archives that were missing from the earlier event run were subsequently retrieved. An independent re-download through the research loader returned 288 validated 5-minute rows for each of VVVUSDT, WIFUSDT, XANUSDT and ZECUSDT. Once the repaired event files were in place, the event totals independently reconstructed from baseline research labels exactly matched the original extraction for every one of the five timeframes and both directions (10 comparisons). The research evaluation must still be interpreted in light of warm-up limitations, candidate-universe survivorship, floating-point precision and the trading-model limitations above.

On the completed 100-symbol aggregate, 10 configurations were selected on train, five passed validation, and one met the explicitly recorded exploratory holdout gate: Bollinger-band reversal (20, 2), 4h LONG, 1,994 holdout trades and mean net return 0.00149755 per trade under the assumed round-trip costs. This is an exploratory association, not independent confirmation or evidence of deployable profitability. Interim holdout views, multiple comparisons, correlated samples, absence of funding, and lack of a shared-capital model prevent that claim. The required checks of robust performance by month and symbol have not been implemented. Ten lab tests passed after the full run.

## Enrichment review (in progress, 50-symbol snapshot)

At review, 50 event-feature metadata files covered 201,497 events: 201,472 marked complete and 25 with incomplete indicator warm-up. Metadata counts reconciled; a sample across five files placed each indicator candle immediately before its event candle. Inspection found causal prior-candle signals, gap resets, and null rather than neutral for missing warm-up. Event annotations do not independently prove that all event OHLC values match a newly retrieved archive; the earlier event-count reconciliation remains a count-level check.

Fifty risk-sensitivity checkpoints were present; their stop, take and ambiguous counts were internally consistent. Synthetic LONG and SHORT bars crossing both 1% stop and 2% target exited at the stop first. The code enters at the next open and otherwise exits at the same candle close. MFE and MAE use full-candle extrema and can occur after a stop or take exit; they are bounds, not realized excursion before exit. Fees/slippage are a fixed 0.1% per round trip; funding, latency and shared capital are omitted. Since this fixed TP/SL sensitivity was added after interim holdout was visible, all its holdout findings are exploratory.

Checkpoint limitation: the risk cache fingerprint includes the shared benchmark, data and indicator files and candidate selection, but does not hash `risk_backtest.py` itself. A later risk-code change requires an explicit version bump or a separate rerun, or stale results could be reused. Thirteen discovered Lab tests plus the two risk aggregation self-tests passed at this review. Processing was still active; counts are a snapshot and not final coverage.

## Final enrichment audit

After all 100 symbols completed, an independent exhaustive scan parsed every compressed event-feature file and corresponding original event file. All 463,220 event IDs matched one-to-one without duplicates; source gzip SHA-256 values matched the feature metadata. For every annotation, symbol, timeframe, open time and direction matched the original event, the decision time equaled the event open, and the feature candle opened exactly one timeframe earlier. Of these events, 463,130 had all implemented indicator signals available and 90 were marked `warmup_incomplete`; these 90 are not zero or neutral values. This audit verifies alignment and identifiers, not every indicator value against a separate implementation.

All 100 risk checkpoints and all 15 risk-summary rows were checked: stop, take and ambiguity counts and pooled gross/net return sums reconciled with the underlying segment records. No inconsistency was found. The fixed 1% stop / 2% take sensitivity has negative pooled holdout mean net returns for all five validation-selected configurations, including Bollinger reversal 4h LONG at -0.00196666 per trade over 1,994 trades. It is an additional exploratory simulation, introduced after interim holdout observations; it neither overturns nor confirms a deployable strategy. Full-bar MFE/MAE remain bounds rather than realized pre-exit excursions, with fixed execution-cost, no funding, and no portfolio-capital model. Thirteen Lab unit tests and two risk aggregation self-tests passed in the final independent review.

The launcher now maintains an external `risk_code.sha256` guard for future resume operations. The existing checkpoint metadata itself does not include the risk code hash, and the run began before the guard; do not represent the risk checkpoints as individually code-hash verified. The guard and a version bump or full rerun are needed for later changes.
